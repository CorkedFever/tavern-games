A TABLE IN THE TAVERN

PULL UP A CHAIR.

Tavern Games is a Dalamud plugin that puts a table of tavern games in your Final Fantasy XIV window. Liar's Dice, Pig, Mia, Blackjack, Texas Hold'em, Roulette, and Ship, Captain and Crew. Play against bots on your own, or open a table and hand friends a four-letter code. Venues host tables for their members and keep a leaderboard. Wagers are flavour: the plugin never touches gil.

THE GAMES

TABLE 1

Liar's Dice

2 to 6 seats

Everyone rolls under a cup. Raise the bid, or call liar and every cup comes off. Lose a die when you're wrong; the last one with dice wins.

TABLE 2

Pig

2 to 6 seats

Roll one die as often as you dare and bank the pot when your nerve goes. A one loses the lot. First to the target wins.

TABLE 3

Mia

2 to 6 seats

Two dice under a cup only you can see, and a number you announce that has to beat the last one. Truthfully or otherwise. Twenty-one is Mia and beats everything.

TABLE 4

Blackjack

1 to 5 seats and the house

Beat the dealer to twenty-one. Hit, stand or double down; the dealer draws to seventeen. Biggest stack after the last round wins.

TABLE 5

Texas Hold'em

2 to 6 seats

A no-limit sit-and-go. Two cards each, five on the board, blinds that climb, side pots that pay only what each seat covered. Play until one seat holds every chip.

TABLE 6

Roulette

1 to 6 seats and the house

Chips on the layout, the wheel turns, the ball lands where the server said it would. Straight numbers pay thirty-five to one; a zero takes every outside bet.

TABLE 7

Ship, Captain and Crew

2 to 6 seats

Five dice, three throws. A six, then a five, then a four, in that order, and the other two dice are your cargo. Best cargo takes the round.

INSTALL

It installs through Dalamud's plugin installer like any other plugin. Two minutes.

Add the repository

In game, open Dalamud settings (/xlsettings), go to Experimental, then Custom Plugin Repositories. Paste this, press the plus, and save.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CorkedFever/tavern-games/main/repo.json

Install the plugin

Open the plugin installer (/xlplugins), search for Tavern Games, install it.

Sit down

/tavern opens the window. Pick a game, press Play vs bots, and you're dealt in. No server, no account.

THE WINDOW

Your seat on the left, the table beside it. The felt shows what everyone sees; your seat shows what only you see.

YOUR TURN LIAR! THE BID HELD POT 260 VICTORY

Your seat

A display strip that says what the table wants of you, your name, your dice or your cards, and the keys for what you can do. It never moves.

The felt

A plate for every seat, lit for whoever is on the clock, and the middle of the table: the standing bid, the pot and board, the wheel.

Fold it

Press the _ in the title bar and only your seat stays, so a game can be played from a corner of the screen while the roleplay carries on. Setup can fold it for you when a game starts.

Staged, not logged

Dice tumble, cards are dealt and turned, chips fly to the winner, a call stamps the felt, and the end of a game is confetti or a red flush. Sounds are the plugin's own; both can be switched off.

WITH OTHERS

Playing alone needs nothing. Playing with people needs a server, and the tavern runs one.

Connect

Under Setup, connect to the tavern's server. The first time, the server gives your install a profile it will recognise again. No account, no password.

Open a table, or join one

A table gets a four-letter code. Friends enter it under Join a code to sit down, or watch from the rail. The server rolls every die and deals every card, and tells each seat only what that seat may see, so a tampered client cannot cheat.

Venues

Anyone with a profile can start a venue and hand out its join code. Staff host tables for it, members find those tables on the venue's page, and every finished game counts on its leaderboard. Games against bots don't.
